Real-world check: Ireland, 2020 Of 4,192 referrals produced by voluntary scanning and sent to the Irish police, only 852 (about 20%) were abuse material; 471 (about 11%) were confirmed false positives. Source: ICCL / EDRi (2022), An Garda Síochána data.
All rates above are illustrative assumptions: no EU-wide error rates for message scanning are published. Presets are rough orders of magnitude, not measurements of any product.
